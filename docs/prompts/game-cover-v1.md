Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: official landscape game cover and repository preview image
Input images: Image 1 is the mandatory visual identity and pixel-art style

Style/medium: handcrafted high-quality 2D pixel art matching Image 1, crisp hard

Composition/framing: wide landscape cover, approximately 16:9. Strong
bottom-to-top diagonal ascent. Byte is the clear foreground focal point. The

Lighting/mood: rusty warm light at the scrapyard transitions upward into violet
atmosphere and cool hopeful cyan moonlight; adventurous, challenging,
emotional, ultimately hopeful.

Color palette: preserve charcoal #242733, rust orange #C76636, warm cream
#F1D6A4, cyan #63D6D2, lunar blue #7EA5D8, violet #67548E, danger red #D94A4A.

Text (verbatim): "LITTLE BOLT, BIG MOON"

Typography: large readable custom pixel-art title centered across the upper
dark-sky area; warm cream letters with restrained cyan highlights; render the
title exactly once and exactly as written, including comma; no subtitle and no
other words.

Constraints: preserve Byte's design and badge exactly; visually communicate
vertical climbing, dangerous falling height, scrapyard-to-moon journey, and a
sweet reunion goal; keep the cover readable at thumbnail size; no logos, no
trademarks, no watermark.

Avoid: existing copyrighted characters, horizontal side-scroller composition,
excessive weapons, gore, realistic humans, glossy 3D, smooth vector art, tiny
unreadable text, extra titles, misspelled title, clutter.
